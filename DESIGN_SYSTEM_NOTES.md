- **Weights Available:** Regular (400), Medium (500), Semi Bold (600)

- **Display 10:** 3.2rem, line-height 1.15em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 9:** 2.8rem, line-height 1.15em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 8:** 2.5rem, line-height 1.25em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 7:** 2.25rem, line-height 1.25em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 6:** 1.875rem, line-height 1.25em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 5:** 1.5rem, line-height 1.25em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 4:** 1.25rem, line-height 1.25em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 3:** 1.125rem, line-height 1.25em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 2:** 1rem, line-height 1.25em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold
- **Display 1:** 0.875rem, line-height 1.25em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold

### Paragraph Styles

- **Paragraph Default:** 1rem, line-height 1.5em, weights: Default/Medium/Semibold

## Color Palette - Neutral Colors
- **Neutral 100:** #FFFFFF (white)
- **Neutral 200:** #FBFBFB (very light gray)
- **Neutral 300:** #F3F3F3 (light gray)
- **Neutral 400:** #E3E3E3 (slightly darker light gray)
- **Neutral 500:** #ABABAB (medium gray)
- **Neutral 600:** #505050 (dark gray)
- **Neutral 700:** #292929 (very dark gray)
- **Neutral 800:** #161616 (black)

## Accent Colors
- **Accent 1:** #D1B4C6 (soft muted purple/dusty rose - warm undertone)
- **Accent 2:** #CBC4D6 (soft muted lavender/light periwinkle - cool undertone)

## Buttons

### Primary Button
- **Background:** Black (#000000 or #161616)
- **Text:** White, uppercase
- **Sizes:** SMALL, DEFAULT
- **Content Types:** TEXT, ICON, NUMBER
- **Text buttons include:** "DEFAULT >" (with right-pointing chevron)
- **Style:** Rounded corners, solid black background

### Secondary Button
- **Background:** White
- **Text:** Black, uppercase
- **Border:** Black border (thin)
- **Sizes:** SMALL, DEFAULT
- **Content Types:** TEXT, ICON, NUMBER
- **Text buttons include:** "DEFAULT >" (with right-pointing chevron)
- **Style:** Rounded corners, white background with black border

## Design Principles
- Clean, minimalist design
- Black and white color scheme
- Consistent spacing and alignment
- Rounded corners on buttons
- Uppercase text on buttons
- Right-pointing chevron (>) on text buttons

## Notes for Implementation
- Use Inter Tight font throughout
- Follow the display/paragraph size scale
- Use neutral color palette for backgrounds and text
- Buttons should have rounded corners
- Maintain consistent line heights (1.15em for large displays, 1.25em for smaller displays, 1.5em for paragraphs)
